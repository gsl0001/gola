First Five Minutes

Five steps to master the gesture.

1

Hold the Chord

Hold Ctrl+Alt (or your configured mouse button). The wheel opens immediately centered under your mouse cursor.

2

Slide to a Slice

The inner ring holds your curated apps plus System, Tools, and Shelf. Green dots and numbers show open instances.

3

Outer Ring Fan & Release

Dwell on an app slice with multiple windows open to fan out live window previews. Slide onto a preview and let go to focus it.

4

Shortcuts & Dismiss

Press 1–9 to select a slice directly. Press Esc or release outside to close the wheel.

5

Local Dictation & Telegram Shelf

Focus a text field, hold the chord, and speak — Gola types locally using an offline 141 MB speech model. Send files from your phone via your own Telegram bot.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about Gola.

Is Gola spyware? ▼

No. It installs a system-wide keyboard hook (the only way Windows detects a key being held and released), captures screen content for radial previews, and opens the microphone for dictation.

Keystrokes are compared against your trigger chord and passed straight on, never recorded or transmitted. Screen capture stays local. Microphone opens only while dictating. No account, no telemetry, no servers. Detail in the Privacy Policy.

Why did my antivirus flag it? ▼

Keyboard hooks, screen capture, and running configured commands together match heuristic antivirus detection patterns. False positives happen even though every one of those is required for the launcher to work. The Store build is signed by Microsoft.

The wheel does nothing when I hold the chord ▼

Almost always a trigger conflict. On keyboard layouts that use AltGr (which Windows reports as Ctrl+Alt), the default chord collides with typing. Change it in Settings → Keymap to Ctrl+Shift.

Opening it with the mouse, or a laptop trackpad ▼

Settings → Keymap lets you pick any combination of Back, Forward, and Middle buttons. They are toggles — hold and flick, or tap to keep the wheel open for a click.

Trackpad: Choose Middle, then in Windows Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Touchpad → Three-finger gestures set Taps to "Middle mouse button".

Where does Gola keep my data? ▼

%APPDATA%\Gola\ for settings, and %USERPROFILE%\Gola\ for screenshots, notes, Shelf files, and the speech model.
