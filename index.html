The 5-Minute Guided Tutorial

Mastering Gola takes about five seconds. Here is how the radial launcher works on your desktop.

1

Hold the Chord

Hold Ctrl+Alt (or your chosen mouse trigger). A frosted two-ring radial wheel opens immediately centered right under your mouse pointer.

2

Slide Toward a Slice

The inner ring holds your curated apps alongside three sections: System, Tools, and Shelf. Green dots and numbers indicate running application instances.

3

Outer Ring Fan Out & Release

Dwell for a split second on an app slice with multiple windows open to fan out real live thumbnail previews on the outer ring. Slide onto a specific preview and release to focus it, or release outside to cancel.

4

Direct Key Shortcuts & Mouse Toggles

While the wheel is open, press 1–9 to select a slice directly, or press Esc to close. Mouse Back, Forward, and Middle wheel buttons act as toggles — hold and flick, or tap to lock open.

5

Local Dictation & Telegram Shelf

Focus any text field, hold the trigger chord, and speak — Gola transcribes your speech locally using an offline 141 MB speech model. Send files from your phone directly to your desktop via your own Telegram bot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Gola's architecture, security, privacy, and customization.

Is Gola spyware? ▼

No. Gola installs a low-level system keyboard hook, captures screen content for radial previews, and opens the microphone for dictation. Here is exactly why each is required:

  • Keyboard Hook: It is the only way Windows allows an application to detect a modifier key being held and released. Keypresses are compared against your trigger chord and passed straight on—they are never recorded or transmitted.
  • Screen Capture: Used solely to render the frosted glass backdrop and live window thumbnail previews on your machine.
  • Microphone: Opens only while you actively dictate text into a field. Speech recognition runs 100% locally.

There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics, and no remote servers. For full details, review our Privacy Policy.

Why did my antivirus flag Gola? ▼

Keyboard hooks, screen capturing, and launching user-configured shell commands together match heuristic security detection patterns used by antivirus scanners. False positives happen even though every one of these features is essential for Gola to function as a launcher. The Microsoft Store build is digitally signed by Microsoft. If your security software flags it, reporting it as a false positive helps the community.

The wheel does nothing when I hold the chord ▼

This is almost always a trigger conflict. On keyboard layouts that use AltGr (which Windows internally reports as Ctrl+Alt), the default chord collides with normal typing. Open Gola Settings → Keymap and change the trigger chord to Ctrl+Shift.

How do I open Gola with a mouse or laptop trackpad? ▼

In Settings → Keymap, you can enable any combination of Back, Forward, and Middle mouse buttons as triggers. They are independent toggles, so hold and flick as usual, or tap to keep the wheel open for a click.

Laptop Trackpad Setup: Choose Middle in Gola Settings, then in Windows Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Touchpad → Three-finger gestures, set Taps to "Middle mouse button". Windows sends three-finger taps as middle clicks, triggering Gola.

Note: Gola claims its assigned trigger buttons, so a thumb button will stop browser back/forward while active. If using mouse configuration software (e.g. Logitech Options+), leave the button set to default Back/Forward there so raw hardware inputs reach Gola.

Where does Gola keep my data? ▼

All configuration and local files are stored locally on your machine:

  • %APPDATA%\Gola\ — App settings & keymap configuration
  • %USERPROFILE%\Gola\ — Screenshots, quick notes, Shelf inbox files, and the offline speech recognition model.

Uninstalling Gola removes the app binary; deleting those two folders completely purges all associated data.

What outbound network connections does Gola make? ▼

Gola makes exactly two possible outbound network connections:

  1. The one-off download of the ~141 MB local speech recognition model (only when Dictation is enabled).
  2. Requests to api.telegram.org only if you explicitly supply your own Telegram bot token to connect your mobile Shelf.

Clipboard history is stored strictly in volatile RAM (last 20 items, never written to disk), and content marked by password managers as excluded is automatically skipped.
