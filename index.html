Everything you reach for, one flick away.

It switches. It doesn't relaunch.

The taskbar often opens a fresh empty window while your work sits behind it. Gola shows what's already open, with a green dot on anything running, and takes you straight back to it.

Questions

Why did my antivirus flag it?

A keyboard hook, screen capture for the previews and launching apps together look like what antivirus engines watch for. A hold-and-flick launcher needs all three. The Store build is signed by Microsoft.

Can I open it with the mouse?

Yes. In Settings, pick Back, Forward or Middle and hold it instead of a key chord. On a trackpad, map a three-finger tap to middle-click in Windows settings and choose Middle.

Holding Ctrl+Alt does nothing

Keyboard layouts with an AltGr key report it as Ctrl+Alt. Change the chord in Settings, for example to CtrlShift.

Where is my data kept?

Settings live in %APPDATA%\Gola\. Screenshots, notes and Shelf files live in %USERPROFILE%\Gola\. Nothing is uploaded.
