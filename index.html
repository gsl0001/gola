Held, not clicked.

    Ctrl+Alt, any chord you choose, or a mouse thumb button. The wheel opens where you are pointing.

It switches. It doesn't relaunch.

The taskbar often opens a fresh empty window while your work sits behind it. Gola shows what is already open, with a green dot on anything running and a count of its windows, and takes you straight back to the one you meant. New window is there for when you do want one.

What it touches, and why.

A hold-and-flick launcher needs permissions that deserve a straight answer. No account, no telemetry, no servers.

Keyboard
Keys are checked against your chord and passed straight on. Never recorded, never sent.
Screen
Captured only to draw the live window previews. The images stay on this PC.
Microphone
Only while you dictate, with dictation switched on. Transcribed locally, then discarded.
Clipboard
Feeds clipboard history, kept on your own disk and never uploaded.
Network
The one-off speech-model download, and Telegram only if you set up your own bot.

Every permission is explained in the privacy policy.

Questions

What does it cost?

Gola has a free trial on the Microsoft Store. After that it's US$6.99, paid once, and updates arrive through the Store. There is no subscription.

Why did my antivirus flag it?

A keyboard hook, screen capture for the previews and launching apps together look like what antivirus engines watch for. A hold-and-flick launcher needs all three. The Store build is signed by Microsoft.

Can I open it with the mouse or a trackpad?

Yes. In Settings, pick Back, Forward or Middle and hold it instead of a key chord. Gola takes that button over, so it stops doing browser back or forward. On a trackpad, map a three-finger tap to middle-click in Windows settings and choose Middle.

Holding Ctrl+Alt does nothing

Keyboard layouts with an AltGr key report it as Ctrl+Alt. Change the chord in Settings → Keymap, for example to Ctrl+Shift.

Where is my data kept?

Settings live in %APPDATA%\Gola\. Screenshots, notes and Shelf files live in %USERPROFILE%\Gola\. Nothing is uploaded.
